Our mission is simple: to prepare students not just for exams, but for life.

Frequently Asked Questions
Know More About Brookfield
Find quick answers about our curriculum, admissions, programs, and campus life. Still have questions? Get in touch with us!
1. What curriculum does the school follow?
We offer an internationally recognized curriculum designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and global awareness.
2. Is Islamic education part of the academic program?
Yes, Islamic Theology is an integral part of our curriculum, focusing on values, character-building, and spiritual development.
3. What is Project-Based Learning (PBL)?
PBL is a hands-on approach where students learn by working on real-world projects, encouraging deeper understanding and problem-solving skills.
4. Do students learn coding and robotics at an early age?
Yes, our Coding &amp; Robotics Lab introduces students to programming, logical thinking, and innovation from the primary level onward.
5. What age groups or grades do you currently admit?
We admit students starting from early childhood education through to higher primary/secondary levels. Please contact us for grade-specific openings.
6. How can I apply for admission?
You can fill out the admission enquiry form on our website or visit the school office for more information and assistance.
7. Is there an entrance test or interview?
Depending on the grade level, there may be a basic assessment or interaction to understand your child&#039;s learning needs.
